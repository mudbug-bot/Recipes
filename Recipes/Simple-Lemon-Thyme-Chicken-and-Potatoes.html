Simple Lemon Thyme Chicken and Potatoes

A weeknight chicken dinner that eats like something far fancier, crisp skin, buttery potatoes, a lemon-thyme pan sauce, and a sharp little salad to keep it all in balance.

Serves 2 to 3
Sheet pan + stovetop
350°F oven

Ingredients

For the chicken and potatoes

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1.5 lb baby Dutch yellow potatoes, halved
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme, plus more for finishing
  • Salt

For the pan sauce and finish

  • 1 to 2 tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
  • 1 large Belgian shallot, sliced, or 2 regular shallots
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 3 tbsp cold butter
  • Splash of water
  • Salt and freshly cracked pepper

For the salad

  • 2 to 3 cups wild arugula
  • 5 radishes, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp nutritional yeast
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. On a sheet pan, toss the halved potatoes and chicken thighs with a generous amount of olive oil, thyme, and salt. Lay the chicken skin-side down directly on the pan, with the potatoes scattered around it.
  3. Roast for about 15 minutes, until the chicken skin is starting to crisp where it contacts the pan and the potatoes are picking up golden color.
  4. Remove the pan from the oven. Take the chicken off and set it aside on a plate. Toss the potatoes in the rendered chicken fat and scrape up the browned bits. Return the potatoes to the oven to continue roasting.
  5. Heat a carbon steel or heavy pan over medium-high heat. Add the olive oil or avocado oil.
  6. Place the chicken thighs skin-side down in the pan. Add the sliced shallots around the chicken so they soften and caramelize in the fat.
  7. Cook 2 to 3 minutes to deepen the crust, then flip the chicken.
  8. Add the juice of 1 lemon, the lemon halves directly into the pan, the butter, a small splash of water, salt, pepper, and fresh thyme.
  9. Swirl the butter into the sauce as it melts. Once it emulsifies, kill the heat.
  10. Soak the sliced radishes in cold water for 1 minute, then drain. Toss with arugula, cider vinegar, olive oil, nutritional yeast, salt, and pepper. Keep it light and sharp.
  11. Pull the potatoes from the oven and scrape everything, crispy bits included, into a serving dish. Top with the chicken. Spoon the lemon-thyme butter sauce and caramelized shallots over everything. Serve the salad on the side.

Notes

  • The whole point is render, reuse, and layer flavor instead of piling on more ingredients.
  • Starting the chicken skin-side down on the sheet pan helps crisp the skin and seasons the potatoes with chicken fat right away.
  • The acid at the end keeps the plate from feeling heavy.
If you can run an oven and a pan at the same time, you can make this. That’s the beauty of it.
Why it works: The potatoes get the benefit of rendered chicken fat, the pan sauce is built from browned bits and lemon butter, and the peppery salad cuts straight through the richness.
