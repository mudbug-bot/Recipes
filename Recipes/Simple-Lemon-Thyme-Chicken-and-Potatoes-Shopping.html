Simple Lemon Thyme Chicken and Potatoes

🛒 Must Have

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1.5 lb baby Dutch yellow potatoes
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp cold butter
  • 2 to 3 cups wild arugula
  • 5 radishes

🧄 Aromatics – Check Fridge

  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme, plus more for finishing
  • 1 large Belgian shallot or 2 regular shallots

❓ Optional

  • Avocado oil instead of olive oil for the stovetop finish

🌶️ Spices – Check Pantry First

  • 1 tsp nutritional yeast
  • Salt
  • Freshly cracked black pepper

🥫 Other

  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • Splash of water
