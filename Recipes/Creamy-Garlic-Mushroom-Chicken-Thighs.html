Creamy Garlic Mushroom Chicken Thighs

Pan-fried chicken thighs in a rich garlic mushroom cream sauce, finished with Parmesan and parsley for an easy weeknight dinner.

30 Minutes One Skillet Creamy Mushroom Sauce

Ingredients

  • 6 chicken thighs, skin on
  • 9 oz. cremini mushrooms, or any brown mushrooms of choice
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons neutral cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 2 teaspoons Italian herb blend
  • 1/3 cup chicken stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Fresh parsley, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Wash and pat the chicken thighs dry well.
  2. Season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper on all sides.
  3. Coat the chicken thighs in cornstarch on a large plate, then shake off and discard any excess cornstarch.
  4. Heat the oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Once hot, sear the chicken thighs for about 6 minutes on both sides, until crisp and golden brown.
  5. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover the skillet, and cook for an additional 12 minutes, or until the thickest part of the chicken reaches 165°F. Transfer the chicken to a plate.
  6. In the same skillet, melt the butter and add the sliced mushrooms. Cook, stirring often, for about 4 minutes. Add the Italian herbs and garlic and cook for 30 seconds to 1 minute more. Deglaze with the chicken stock and simmer for 3 minutes. Keep the heat at medium-low and slowly pour in the cream. Let it simmer briefly, then add the Parmesan. Stir until the cheese melts and the sauce thickens. Taste and adjust salt and black pepper.
  7. Return the cooked chicken thighs to the skillet and coat in the sauce. Let them simmer in the sauce for 3 to 5 minutes. Garnish with parsley and more Parmesan.
Good with mashed potatoes, rice, or crusty bread to catch the sauce.
