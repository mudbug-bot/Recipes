Creamy Garlic Mushroom Chicken Thighs

🛒 Must Have

  • Chicken thighs, skin on – 6
  • Cremini mushrooms – 9 oz.
  • Cornstarch – 1/2 cup
  • Chicken stock – 1/3 cup
  • Heavy cream – 1 cup
  • Parmesan cheese, grated – 1/3 cup
  • Neutral cooking oil – 2 tablespoons
  • Butter – 1 tablespoon

🧄 Aromatics – Check Fridge

  • Garlic – 1 tablespoon minced
  • Fresh parsley – for garnish

❓ Optional

  • Extra Parmesan for serving
  • Crusty bread, rice, or mashed potatoes for serving

🌶️ Spices – Check Pantry First

  • Salt – 1 teaspoon
  • Black pepper – 1/2 teaspoon
  • Italian herb blend – 2 teaspoons

🥫 Other

  • Any brown mushrooms can substitute for cremini
